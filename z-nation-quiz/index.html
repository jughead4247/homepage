About the Z Nation Quiz

The Z Nation Quiz is a 30-question trivia challenge for fans of the post-apocalyptic television series Z Nation. It tests how well you remember the characters, zombies, missions, locations and memorable events from the series.

Questions include major characters such as Murphy, Warren, 10K, Citizen Z, Addy and Garnett, along with details about the dangerous world they travel through. The quiz also includes questions about zombies, important missions, locations and events that fans may remember from watching the series.

Whether you watched Z Nation casually or followed the characters and their journey closely, the quiz gives you a chance to test your memory. Some questions focus on familiar characters and major events, while others require you to remember specific details from the show.

Z Nation Series Details

Z Nation is a post-apocalyptic television series centered on a group of survivors attempting to transport Murphy, a man who may hold the key to a possible cure, across a zombie-infested United States.

The series follows characters including Roberta Warren, Murphy, 10K, Addy, Doc, Citizen Z and other survivors as they encounter zombies, hostile groups, strange mutations and dangerous communities.

Across its story, the survivors encounter locations, organizations and events that continually change the nature of the zombie apocalypse.

What This Quiz Covers

Questions cover major characters including Murphy, Warren, 10K, Citizen Z, Addy, Garnett, Vasquez, Kurian and other important figures.

The quiz also covers zombies, zombie-human hybrids, missions, locations, military operations, survivor groups and important events throughout the series.

Some questions focus on memorable details that may be easier to forget, making the quiz suitable for viewers who followed the series closely.

How the Z Nation Quiz Works

The quiz contains 30 questions. Each question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before continuing.

Every correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. Your score is calculated from your total correct answers and converted into a percentage. The highest possible result is 100%.

Your final percentage represents your level of knowledge about Z Nation. A high score means you remember a large number of characters, events and details from the series, while a lower score means there may be more of the zombie-filled world left to rediscover.

Quiz Navigation

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an answer

Choose the answer you think is correct. Take your time to read the question and all four choices, especially when several answers seem familiar.

Next and Back buttons

Use Next to move forward through the quiz. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review your previous selection. Your progress and current question number are displayed while you play.

Finishing the quiz

Continue through all 30 questions and answer every question. When you reach the end, the quiz will allow you to submit your answers and view your final percentage and Z Nation knowledge result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After seeing your result, select TRY AGAIN if you want another attempt. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your achievement and see whether other fans can beat your score.
