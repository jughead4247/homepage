About A Quiet Place Movie Quiz

The A Quiet Place Movie Quiz is a 25-question trivia challenge based on the 2018 science-fiction horror film A Quiet Place. The story follows the Abbott family as they try to survive in a world where mysterious creatures hunt anything that makes a sound.

The movie combines survival horror, family drama and science fiction as Lee, Evelyn, Regan and Marcus attempt to protect one another while learning how to live under the constant threat of the creatures.

This quiz focuses on the movie's characters, locations, survival methods, important events and details about the creatures. It is designed for viewers who want to test how closely they remember the Abbott family's struggle to survive.

A Quiet Place Movie Details

Release year: 2018

Director: John Krasinski

Genre: Science fiction, horror and thriller

Main setting: The Abbott family's isolated farm and surrounding area

Main characters: Lee Abbott, Evelyn Abbott, Regan Abbott and Marcus Abbott

Key survival rule: The creatures hunt by detecting sound.

Important technology: Regan's cochlear implant and its unusual effect on the creatures

What This Quiz Covers

The questions are based on details shown or established in A Quiet Place. Some questions focus on the Abbott family and their relationships, while others test your memory of specific events and survival techniques.

The Abbott Family

Questions may cover Lee, Evelyn, Regan, Marcus, Beau and the relationships between the members of the Abbott family.

Survival Methods

The quiz includes details about the family's methods of surviving in a world where making noise can attract the creatures.

The Creatures

Questions can test your memory of the creatures' behavior, hearing abilities, appearance and vulnerabilities.

Important Events

The quiz covers major events from the movie, including the opening supply trip, the accident involving Beau, the birth of Evelyn's baby, Lee's sacrifice and the family's final discovery.

Regan's Hearing Device

Regan's cochlear implant becomes an important part of the family's understanding of how the creatures react to certain sounds. Questions may test your memory of its role in the story.

How Quiz Works

The quiz contains 25 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 25 questions in total, your correct answers are converted into a final percentage.

Your final result represents how many questions you answered correctly. The result categories are based on your performance across the 25 questions.

Quiz Navigation

Finishing the Quiz

FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 25 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers A Quiet Place, including the Abbott family, Lee, Evelyn, Regan, Marcus, the creatures, survival methods, the farm, important events and the role of Regan's hearing device.

Is this quiz only about the characters?

No. Character questions are only part of the quiz. Other questions cover survival techniques, the creatures, locations, technology, important events and major plot details.

Which movie does this quiz cover?

This quiz focuses on the 2018 science-fiction horror film A Quiet Place, directed by John Krasinski.

Do I need to watch the movie before taking the quiz?

It is recommended. The questions are based on events, characters and details from the movie, including information that may be difficult to answer without having watched it.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 25 questions you answered correctly. A higher percentage means you correctly remembered more of the movie's details.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.
