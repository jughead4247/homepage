About A Quiet Place Part II Movie Quiz

The A Quiet Place Part II Movie Quiz is a 25-question trivia challenge based on the 2020 horror film A Quiet Place Part II. The story follows Regan and Marcus Abbott, their mother Evelyn, and Emmett as they attempt to survive in a world where mysterious creatures hunt anything that makes a sound.

The movie expands the story introduced in A Quiet Place and follows the Abbott family after they leave their home. Regan discovers information about a possible safe location while Emmett helps her pursue the mysterious radio signal.

This quiz focuses on the movie's characters, locations, survival methods, creatures, radio signal, major events and important story details. It is designed for viewers who want to test how closely they remember the events of A Quiet Place Part II.

A Quiet Place Part II Movie Details

Release year: 2020

Director: John Krasinski

Genre: Horror, science fiction and thriller

Main characters: Regan Abbott, Marcus Abbott, Evelyn Abbott and Emmett

Main setting: The Abbott family's journey through abandoned locations and survivor communities

Key survival element: Sound attracts the creatures, making silence essential for survival.

Story connection: The film continues the story of A Quiet Place.

What This Quiz Covers

The questions are based on details shown or established in A Quiet Place Part II. Some questions focus on characters and their relationships, while others test your memory of specific events and survival situations.

Characters

Questions may cover Regan Abbott, Marcus Abbott, Evelyn Abbott, Emmett and other characters who play important roles in the story.

The Creatures

The quiz includes questions about the creatures, how they hunt, their sensitivity to sound and the methods survivors use to defend themselves.

Regan and the Radio Signal

Regan investigates a repeating radio broadcast and believes it may lead to a safe location. Questions can test your memory of the signal, the song being played and Regan's journey.

The Island

The island becomes an important location in the story. The quiz covers why survivors believed it was safe and what happens when the creatures eventually reach it.

Survival and Major Events

Questions may cover the opening sequence, the Abbott family's journey, the foundry, the marina, the island and the major dangers faced by the characters.

How Quiz Works

The quiz contains 25 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 25 questions in total, your correct answers are converted into a final percentage.

Your final result represents how many questions you answered correctly. The result categories are based on your performance across the 25 questions.
