About the Fallout TV Show Quiz

The Fallout TV Show Quiz is a 50-question trivia challenge for viewers of the television series Fallout. It tests how well you remember the characters, Vaults, locations, factions, occupations, identities and important events introduced throughout the show.

The questions explore the strange and dangerous world of the Wasteland, from life inside the Vaults to the people and factions encountered outside them. You will face questions about Lucy, the Ghoul, Maximus, the Brotherhood of Steel, Vault-Tec, Vault 33 and other important parts of the story.

The quiz is designed for both casual viewers and dedicated Fallout fans. Some questions focus on memorable characters and major events, while others test smaller details such as names, places, occupations and information revealed during the series.

Fallout TV Show Details

This quiz is based on the Fallout television series and focuses on story details presented in the show.

Questions cover the Wasteland, Vaults, factions, characters, important locations, pre-war events, discoveries and major developments throughout the series.

The quiz also includes questions about important Fallout terminology and elements connected to the wider Fallout universe when they appear in the show.

What This Quiz Covers

Characters

Lucy, Cooper Howard, Maximus, Hank, Moldaver, Barb and other important characters.

Vault 31, Vault 32, Vault 33 and the Vault-Tec experiments surrounding them.

Factions

The Brotherhood of Steel, NCR, Vault-Tec and other groups encountered throughout the story.

Locations and Events

Shady Sands, Griffith Observatory, the Wasteland, major discoveries and important events from the series.

How the Fallout Quiz Works

The quiz contains 50 questions, and every question has four possible choices. Only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe matches what happened in the television series.

A correct answer gives you 2 points, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 50 questions in total, the maximum possible score is 100 points.

Your final score is used to determine your Fallout Knowledge level. A high score means you remembered a large amount of the show's characters, events and details, while a lower score may reveal areas of the Wasteland you need to revisit.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question and its four possible answers will then appear.

Selecting an answer

Select the answer you believe is correct before continuing to the next question. Your choices are kept as you move through the quiz, allowing you to review earlier questions.

Next and Back buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button lets you return to an earlier question and review or change your answer. Your current question number and overall progress are displayed during the quiz.

Finishing the quiz

Continue until you have answered all 50 questions. Once all required answers have been selected, use the submit option to finish the quiz and view your final score and Fallout Knowledge result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After seeing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result and challenge other fans.

Fallout Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 50 questions, with four possible choices for each question.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100 points. Each correct answer is worth 2 points.

What topics are covered?

Questions cover characters, Vaults, locations, factions, occupations, identities, major events and other details from the Fallout television series.

Is the quiz difficult?

The quiz includes both major story details and smaller facts from the series, so some questions should be easier for casual viewers while others may challenge dedicated fans.

What does my final score mean?

Your score represents how many questions you answered correctly and determines your Fallout Knowledge level.

Can I replay the quiz?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your Fallout score.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz contains spoilers for the Fallout television series. Some questions may reveal important characters, events, relationships, locations, discoveries and other story details.

If you have not finished watching the relevant episodes or season, consider completing them before taking the quiz to avoid having important plot details spoiled.
