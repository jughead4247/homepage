About this Survival Test

The Ultimate Zombie Apocalypse Survival Test is a 100-decision fictional survival challenge set during a developing zombie outbreak. Instead of testing knowledge of a particular movie or television series, it asks you to make choices as conditions change around you.

The situations cover food, water, shelter, transportation, medical problems, dangerous encounters, resources, leadership and relationships with other survivors. Some decisions involve immediate danger, while others require planning for the days and weeks ahead.

Your decisions determine your Survival Score. Certain questions also contribute to a separate Morality Score, measuring two different aspects of your choices rather than treating survival and morality as the same thing.

This is a fictional entertainment experience and thought experiment. Your result is not a scientific prediction of how you would perform in a real emergency or disaster.

What This Test Covers

The 100 decisions cover problems that could arise during a fictional zombie outbreak. Some involve immediate threats; others focus on managing limited resources and making decisions that affect your group over time.

Food and Water

Find, protect and manage essential supplies when access to food and clean water becomes limited.

Shelter and Transportation

Choose secure locations, move between areas and decide when transportation is worth the risk.

Medical Problems

Respond to injuries, illness and limited medical supplies when professional help is unavailable.

Dangerous Encounters

Face zombies, dangerous environments and other threats where knowing when to hide, escape or take a risk can affect survival.

Other Survivors

Consider trust, cooperation, leadership, conflict and relationships with other survivors.

Morality and Difficult Choices

Some decisions score compassion, fairness, trust, responsibility and willingness to help others when doing so may create risks for the group.

How the Test Works

The test contains 100 fictional survival decisions, each with four possible choices. Read the situation and select the option you think offers the best outcome within that scenario.

Each decision can award up to 5 raw Survival points, for a maximum possible raw total of 500. The result screen converts your total to a percentage using the scoring range available in the question bank.

Some decisions also include a morality component. These contribute separately to your Morality Score and can reflect compassion, fairness, trust, responsibility and willingness to help others.

The two scores are separate. A player can make effective survival choices while scoring lower on morality. A compassionate choice is not necessarily the safest choice in every fictional situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many decisions are in the test?

There are 100 fictional survival decisions, each with four possible choices.

What is the maximum Survival Score?

Each decision can award up to 5 raw Survival points, for a maximum possible raw total of 500. The displayed Survival Score is normalized to a percentage based on the scoring range available in the question bank.

What is the Morality Score?

The Morality Score is calculated separately from questions with a morality component. It reflects patterns in choices involving compassion, fairness, trust and responsibility.

Can I have a high Survival Score and a low Morality Score?

Yes. The two scores measure different aspects of your decisions. A choice that improves survival does not necessarily represent the most compassionate or fair decision.

How are the choices scored?

This is a fictional decision-making experience, not a scientific examination. Each option has point values that affect your Survival Score and, where applicable, your Morality Score.

Is this a real survival test?

No. Zombies and the outbreak are fictional. Your result is for entertainment and does not predict how you would perform during a real emergency or disaster.

Is the test based on a particular zombie series?

No. The test is not based on one specific television series, movie or game. Its scenarios use general themes from zombie and post-apocalyptic fiction.

Can I change my answers?

Yes. Use BACK to return to earlier decisions and change your choices before submitting.

Can I take the test again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to restart and see how different choices affect your scores.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. After completing the test, use CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share the challenge and compare your results.
