About the Plague Survival Test

The Plague Survival Test is a 50-decision fictional challenge set during a developing infectious-disease outbreak and related emergency scenarios. It asks you to make difficult choices as conditions change.

Situations cover infection risk, food, water, shelter, transportation, medicine, emergencies, information, leadership and relationships with other survivors. Some questions focus on immediate danger; others require planning for later stages.

Your choices determine a Survival Score. Certain decisions also contribute to a separate Morality Score, representing two different aspects of your choices.

This is an entertainment experience and thought experiment, not a scientific prediction or a substitute for official emergency guidance or professional medical advice.

What This Test Covers

The 50 decisions explore problems that could arise during an outbreak or a wider disaster, from immediate safety to managing limited resources over time.

Infection Control

Reduce exposure, respond to symptoms, separate potentially infected people when appropriate, maintain hygiene and make informed contact decisions.

Food and Water

Find, protect and manage essential supplies when access to safe food and water becomes limited.

Shelter and Transportation

Choose safer locations, assess travel risks and respond to hazards that may make a shelter unsafe.

Medicine and Healthcare

Respond to illness and injuries, manage limited medical supplies and seek appropriate help when healthcare services are strained.

Information and Emergency Planning

Evaluate rumors, use reliable updates and make decisions when information or communications are limited.

Other Survivors

Consider trust, cooperation, leadership, conflict and responsibilities toward other people.

Morality and Difficult Choices

Some decisions consider compassion, fairness, trust and responsibility when helping others may also create risks for your group.

How the Test Works

The test contains 50 fictional decisions, each with four possible choices. Read the situation and select the option you think is best within that scenario.

Each answer contributes to the Survival Score. Some answers also contribute to the separate Morality Score. Both are displayed as percentages based on the quiz's scoring system.

The scores are intentionally separate: a practical choice is not always the most compassionate, and a compassionate choice is not always the safest in every fictional situation.

Your result includes a decision profile, not a prediction of how long you would survive in a real outbreak.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many decisions are in the test?

There are 50 fictional survival decisions, each with four possible choices.

How is the Survival Score calculated?

Your choices earn points based on the quiz's scoring system for the situations presented. The final score is shown as a percentage and is not a real-world survival prediction.

What does the Morality Score measure?

It reflects choices involving compassion, fairness, responsibility, loyalty and helping others. It does not determine whether you are a good or bad person.

Can I have a high Survival Score and a lower Morality Score?

Yes. The two scores measure different aspects of your choices.

Does the test predict how long I would survive?

No. The result is a fictional decision profile, not a scientific estimate of survival time.

Is this test medically accurate?

No. It is an entertainment experience, not medical advice or a professional emergency-planning tool. Follow qualified medical and public-health authorities during a real outbreak.

Can I change my answers?

Yes. Use BACK to revisit earlier decisions before submitting.

Can I take the test again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after viewing your result.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share the quiz and compare results.
