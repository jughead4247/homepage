Questions & Feedback

We welcome questions and feedback about the quizzes, articles, survival guides, website features, and overall user experience.

You can contact us to report an issue, share your experience, suggest an improvement, or point out something that could make the website more useful and enjoyable.

Quiz Issues and Corrections

If you notice an incorrect answer, a scoring problem, a broken question, a result that does not display correctly, or any other issue with a quiz, please let us know.

When reporting a quiz problem, it helps to include:

  • The quiz name or its URL.
  • The question or result where the problem occurred.
  • A description of what happened and what you expected.
  • Your browser and device, if the problem is technical.

Screenshots can also be useful when reporting visual problems or errors.

Our Articles & Survival Guides section covers survival, emergency preparedness, apocalypse scenarios, science, hypothetical events, and related subjects.

If you notice an inaccurate statement, missing information, outdated material, or something that deserves further explanation, please include the article URL and describe the issue.

Where possible, you can also include a reliable source or reference supporting your suggested correction.

About Apocalypse Quizzes
