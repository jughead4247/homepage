About The Quiz

The Game of Thrones Season 2 Quiz is a 35-question trivia challenge based on the second season of the HBO series Game of Thrones.

Season 2 follows the growing conflict between the claimants to the Iron Throne while several major storylines unfold across Westeros and Essos. The quiz covers Robb Stark, Tyrion Lannister, Arya Stark, Jon Snow, Daenerys Targaryen, Theon Greyjoy and other major characters.

Questions cover major Season 2 events including King's Landing, the Iron Islands, Winterfell, Harrenhal, the Night's Watch, Qarth and the Battle of the Blackwater.

Game of Thrones Season 2 Details

Series: Game of Thrones

Season: 2

Episodes: 10

Based on: A Clash of Kings by George R. R. Martin

Major storylines: Robb Stark's war, Tyrion in King's Landing, Arya at Harrenhal, Jon Snow beyond the Wall, Theon's attack on Winterfell and Daenerys in Qarth.

How Quiz Works

The quiz contains 35 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. Your score is converted into a final percentage.

Your final result represents how many of the Season 2 questions you answered correctly.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the Quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first Season 2 question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an Answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Once you choose an answer, continue to the next question.

Next and Back Buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review or change your selection.

Finishing the Quiz

Continue until all 35 questions have been answered. When you reach the end, submit your answers to finish the quiz and view your final score.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your score.
