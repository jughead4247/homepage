ABOUT THE QUIZ

The Game of Thrones Season 1 Quiz is a 30-question trivia challenge for fans of the HBO fantasy drama. The first season introduces the major houses, characters, political conflicts and threats that shape the world of Westeros.

The questions cover the Stark family, House Lannister, the Night's Watch, King's Landing, Daenerys Targaryen, the Dothraki and the major political events of Season 1.

The quiz also tests memorable locations, titles, relationships, character details and famous dialogue from the first season.

WHAT THIS QUIZ COVERS

The questions cover important characters, houses, locations, political positions, relationships, terminology and major events from Season 1.

The Starks and Winterfell

Questions include Bran Stark, Robb Stark, Ned Stark, Arya Stark, the direwolves and important figures at Winterfell.

The Night's Watch

The quiz covers the Wall, Castle Black, Jeor Mormont, Benjen Stark and other members of the Night's Watch.

King's Landing and Political Intrigue

Questions cover Petyr Baelish, Varys, Ned Stark, the Small Council, the Lannisters and the major revelations surrounding the royal family.

Daenerys and the Dothraki

The quiz covers Viserys Targaryen, Khal Drogo, Jorah Mormont, Illyrio Mopatis, Mirri Maz Duur, Bloodriders and the Dothraki.

Memorable Dialogue

Several questions test famous Season 1 lines from characters including Ned Stark, Tyrion Lannister, Cersei Lannister and Jaime Lannister.

HOW THE QUIZ WORKS

The quiz contains 30 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before continuing.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 30 questions in total, your number of correct answers is converted into a final percentage out of 100%.

Your final result represents your knowledge of Game of Thrones Season 1 based on your performance.
