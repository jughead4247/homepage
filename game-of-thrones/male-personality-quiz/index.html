Which Game of Thrones Male Character Are You?

The Game of Thrones Male Personality Quiz is an interactive character-matching experience inspired by the world of Game of Thrones. Rather than asking which character you like most, it presents fictional dilemmas and asks how you would respond.

Would you uphold an oath when it costs you everything? Use intelligence to outmaneuver a rival? Protect your allies at personal risk? Pursue power, seek revenge or do whatever it takes to survive?

Your answers reveal different combinations of values, motivations and behavioral tendencies. The quiz considers honor, ambition, loyalty, compassion, strategy, ruthlessness and other personality dimensions.

After 50 questions, your answers form a profile across 23 traits. The scoring system compares that profile with 33 male character models to calculate your closest matches.

This is an entertainment quiz inspired by fictional characters, not a psychological or scientific assessment.

How Does the Game of Thrones Male Personality Quiz Work?

The quiz contains 50 questions covering fictional Game of Thrones scenarios, everyday personality decisions and lighter choices inspired by life in Westeros. Each question offers five possible responses.

Each answer contributes to three personality traits and one hidden behavioral route. The route system captures broader approaches such as duty, protection, power, cunning, strategy, survival, freedom, warrior instincts, compassion and vengeance.

No single answer determines the result. Instead, the scoring engine considers your overall profile and compares it with every character's trait scores and route affinities.

At the end, you receive your closest character match, your top three matches, five strongest traits, five lowest traits and your full 23-trait profile.

How to Take the Quiz

Select START QUIZ to begin. Each question presents a situation and five possible answers. Choose the response that best represents what you would do.

The questions appear in a randomized order for each new attempt. The answer choices and their scoring remain attached to their original questions.

Use BACK to revisit a previous question or change an answer. Use NEXT to move forward. You must answer all 50 questions before submitting.

When you finish, select SEE MY RESULT to view your character match and personality profile. You can then share your result, challenge friends or try the quiz again with different choices.

What Does the Game of Thrones Male Personality Quiz Measure?

The quiz measures 23 fictional personality traits. These dimensions help distinguish characters who may share similar goals but approach them in very different ways.

Honor

Your commitment to oaths, principles and personal integrity, even when doing the honorable thing carries a cost.

Courage

Your willingness to face danger, uncertainty and difficult situations rather than automatically retreating.

Loyalty

The importance you place on commitments to friends, family, allies and causes you have chosen to support.

Compassion

How much you consider other people's suffering, needs and circumstances when making difficult decisions.

Morality

How strongly your choices are guided by your beliefs about right, wrong, fairness and responsibility.

Pragmatism

Your tendency to focus on practical outcomes when ideal solutions are unavailable.

Ruthlessness

Your willingness to use harsh or uncompromising methods when you believe the situation demands them.

Ambition

Your desire for achievement, influence, status and greater control over your future.

Leadership

Your willingness to take responsibility, make difficult decisions and guide people through uncertainty.

Independence

How comfortable you are relying on your own judgment instead of following a group or authority.

Intelligence

Your tendency to analyze information, recognize patterns and understand complex situations.

Strategy

Your preference for planning ahead, anticipating consequences and creating advantages before acting.

Manipulation

Your willingness to influence people indirectly or manage information to obtain a desired outcome.

Charisma

Your ability or tendency to influence people through communication, confidence and personal presence.

Pride

How strongly you value dignity, reputation, self-respect and recognition from others.

Determination

Your willingness to keep pursuing a goal despite setbacks, danger or opposition.

Risk Taking

Your willingness to accept uncertainty and possible loss in pursuit of a worthwhile opportunity.

Self-Preservation

How strongly you prioritize your own safety and survival when circumstances become dangerous.

Emotional Control

Your tendency to regulate your reactions, remain composed under pressure and avoid acting purely on emotion.

Curiosity

Your desire to investigate unfamiliar things, ask questions and discover information others might overlook.

Discipline

Your ability to follow routines, maintain standards and control your actions in pursuit of a goal.

Resilience

Your ability to recover from setbacks, adapt after failure and continue when circumstances become difficult.

Impulsiveness

Your tendency to act quickly on instinct or emotion rather than waiting to consider every consequence.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are in the Game of Thrones Male Personality Quiz?

The quiz contains 50 questions. Each question offers five answers, and you must answer all questions to receive your result.

How many characters are included?

The quiz compares your personality with 33 male character profiles, including Jon Snow, Tyrion Lannister, Jaime Lannister, Ned Stark, Tywin Lannister, Petyr Baelish, Varys, Bronn, the Hound and the Night King.

How is my character result calculated?

Your answers create a profile across 23 personality traits. The scoring system compares that profile with the traits and hidden route affinities of each character.

Is this a Game of Thrones trivia quiz?

No. This is a personality quiz, not a knowledge test. The questions use situations inspired by Westeros to explore how you might think and act.

Can I get a different character on another attempt?

Yes. Your questions appear in a randomized order for each attempt, and different answers can change your personality profile and closest character matches.

Why do I receive three character matches?

A personality profile may share traits with several characters. The top three results show your closest matches rather than relying on one character alone.

Is this a scientific personality test?

No. This quiz is designed for entertainment and fictional character matching. It is not a psychological, medical or scientific assessment.

Can I share my result with friends?

Yes. Use the share option after completing the quiz. Your browser or device may open a sharing interface, or the quiz can offer a copyable result and link.

What happens if I refresh before finishing?

Your answers are not guaranteed to be saved between page loads. Refreshing may require you to start the quiz again.
