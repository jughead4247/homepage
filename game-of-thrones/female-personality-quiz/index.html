Which Game of Thrones Female Character Are You?

The Game of Thrones Female Character Quiz is an interactive character-matching experience inspired by the world of Game of Thrones. Instead of asking which character you like most, the quiz places you in difficult fictional situations and asks what you would actually do.

Your decisions can reveal different sides of your personality. You might value honor over political advantage, protect the people closest to you, pursue power, rely on strategy, take dangerous risks, adapt to changing circumstances, or prioritize survival when facing difficult choices.

There is no single correct answer. The questions are designed around difficult choices involving loyalty, power, morality, survival, leadership, ambition, independence and relationships.

After 35 questions, the quiz creates a personality profile using 18 hidden traits. That profile is then compared with 16 female character profiles from Game of Thrones to determine your closest matches.

How Does the Game of Thrones Female Character Quiz Work?

The quiz contains 35 fictional decisions. Every question gives you five possible responses, allowing you to choose the action that best represents what you would do.

Each answer contributes to several hidden personality traits. Different combinations of answers therefore create different personality profiles.

The quiz also tracks broader behavioral approaches through its hidden route system. These routes help distinguish how you respond to questions involving duty, protection, power, strategy, survival, relationships and difficult decisions.

Once all 35 questions are completed, your personality profile is compared against the 16 female character models used by the quiz. Your three closest character matches are displayed on the results page.

Your result is based on the overall pattern of your decisions rather than one answer determining your character.

Quiz Navigation

Select START QUIZ to begin. During the quiz, the current question number and progress bar show where you are in the 35-question experience.

Each question presents a fictional situation with five possible responses. Select the option that best represents what you would do.

Use NEXT to move forward, or BACK to return to an earlier decision and change your answer.

Once all 35 questions have been answered, select SEE MY RESULT to view your character match, top three matches and personality profile.

What Does the Game of Thrones Female Character Quiz Measure?

Your decisions contribute to eighteen fictional personality traits. These traits represent different approaches to leadership, relationships, morality, survival, ambition, strategy and protection.

They are used for entertainment and character matching, not as a psychological or scientific assessment.

Honor

How strongly you value principles, duty and doing what you believe is honorable, even when it creates personal problems.

Courage

Your willingness to confront danger, fear and difficult situations rather than automatically avoiding them.

Loyalty

How strongly you value commitments to people, families, allies and causes you have chosen to support.

Compassion

How much you consider the suffering, emotions and needs of other people when making difficult decisions.

Morality

How strongly your decisions are guided by ideas of right, wrong, justice and personal principles.

Pragmatism

Your tendency to prioritize practical outcomes when ideal solutions are unavailable.

Ruthlessness

Your willingness to use harsh or uncompromising methods when you believe they are necessary.

Ambition

How strongly you seek influence, achievement, status or greater control over your circumstances.

Leadership

Your willingness to take responsibility, make difficult decisions and guide other people.

Independence

How comfortable you are relying on your own judgment rather than following a group or authority.

Intelligence

Your tendency to analyze information, recognize patterns and understand complicated situations.

Strategy

Your preference for planning ahead, considering consequences and creating advantages before acting.

Manipulation

Your willingness to influence people indirectly to achieve a desired outcome.

Charisma

Your tendency to influence people through confidence, communication and personal presence.

Determination

Your willingness to continue pursuing a goal despite setbacks, danger or opposition.

Self-Preservation

How strongly you prioritize your own survival and personal safety when circumstances become dangerous.

Adaptability

How readily you adjust your plans, methods and expectations when circumstances change or familiar approaches stop working.

Protectiveness

How strongly you feel responsible for defending the people you care about and safeguarding those who depend on you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Game of Thrones Female Character Quiz work?

You face 35 fictional situations and choose the response that best represents what you would do. Your choices are analyzed across 18 personality traits and compared with 16 female character profiles.

How many questions are in the quiz?

The quiz contains 35 questions, with five choices available for each question.

How many Game of Thrones female characters are included?

The personality system includes 16 female character profiles: Daenerys Targaryen, Cersei Lannister, Arya Stark, Sansa Stark, Catelyn Stark, Brienne of Tarth, Ygritte, Melisandre, Margaery Tyrell, Yara Greyjoy, Olenna Tyrell, Ellaria Sand, Missandei, Meera Reed, Gilly and Lyanna Mormont.

How is my Game of Thrones female character determined?

Your answers create a personality profile. That profile is compared against the character profiles in the quiz's character-matching and behavioral route system.

Does the quiz depend on Game of Thrones trivia knowledge?

No. This is a personality quiz rather than a knowledge test. The questions focus on fictional decisions and how you would respond to difficult situations.

Can I get a different character on another attempt?

Yes. Changing your decisions can change your personality profile and therefore your closest character matches.

Why do I have three character matches?

Your personality may share important characteristics with several characters. Showing the top three provides a broader view of your closest matches.

Is this a scientific personality test?

No. This is a fictional entertainment experience inspired by Game of Thrones. It is not a psychological, medical or scientific assessment.

Can I share my result with friends?

Yes. After completing the quiz, use the share option when supported by your browser or device. You can also challenge friends to take the quiz.

What happens if I refresh the page during the quiz?

The quiz is designed as an interactive browser experience. If you refresh before finishing, you may need to start again.
