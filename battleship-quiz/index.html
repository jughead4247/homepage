ABOUT THE QUIZ

The Battleship Quiz is a 35-question trivia challenge for fans of the science-fiction action movie. The story follows U.S. Navy Lieutenant Alex Hopper during an alien invasion that begins during the multinational RIMPAC naval exercise in the Pacific.

The questions cover Alex Hopper, Stone Hopper, Sam, Admiral Shane, Yugi Nagata, Mick Canales, Cal Zapata, Walter Lynch and the crews of the American and Japanese ships.

Other questions focus on the alien invasion, Planet G, the communications array, alien technology, the NOAA tsunami buoys, USS Missouri and the major battles around Hawaii.

Battleship Movie Details

Title: Battleship

Format: Science-fiction action film

Setting: The Pacific Ocean and Oʻahu, Hawaii

Main Characters: Alex Hopper, Stone Hopper, Sam Shane, Admiral Shane, Yugi Nagata, Mick Canales, Cal Zapata and Walter Lynch.

The story begins during RIMPAC, a multinational naval exercise in the Pacific. An extraterrestrial force arrives after humans send a signal toward a potentially habitable planet known as Planet G.

WHAT THIS QUIZ COVERS

The questions cover important characters, ships, naval forces, alien technology, battles and major events from Battleship.

Characters and Naval Officers

Questions cover Alex Hopper, Stone Hopper, Sam, Admiral Shane, Yugi Nagata, Mick Canales, Cal Zapata and Walter Lynch.

Ships and Naval Forces

The quiz covers USS John Paul Jones, USS Sampson, JDS Myōkō, USS Missouri and the multinational RIMPAC forces.

Aliens and Alien Technology

Questions cover Planet G, the extraterrestrial signal, the alien spacecraft, Shredders, the aliens' sensitivity to sunlight and their communications technology.

Battles and Events

The quiz covers the initial alien attack, the use of NOAA tsunami warning buoys, the destruction of the alien ships and the battle around Oʻahu.

HOW THE QUIZ WORKS

The quiz contains 35 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before continuing.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 35 questions in total, your number of correct answers is converted into a final percentage out of 100%.

Your final result represents your knowledge of Battleship based on your performance. A high score means you successfully remembered many of the characters, ships, alien technology and events featured in the movie.

FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 35 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What topics are covered?

The quiz focuses on Battleship, including Alex Hopper, Stone Hopper, Sam, Admiral Shane, Yugi Nagata, USS Missouri, the alien invasion, RIMPAC and the final battle.

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.
