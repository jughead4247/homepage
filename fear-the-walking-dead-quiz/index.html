About The Fear the Walking Dead Quiz

The Fear the Walking Dead Quiz is a 50-question trivia challenge based on the AMC television series Fear the Walking Dead. The series begins in Los Angeles during the early stages of the zombie outbreak and follows different groups of survivors as they move through an increasingly dangerous post-apocalyptic world.

The quiz covers characters, communities, factions, locations and major events from across all eight seasons of the series. Questions range from the early outbreak involving the Clark family to later storylines involving Morgan, the Pioneers, the nuclear missile launch, PADRE and the final season.

This quiz is designed for viewers who want to test how closely they remember the characters, locations, major twists and important events of Fear the Walking Dead.

What This Quiz Covers

The questions cover characters, locations, communities, factions and major events from throughout Fear the Walking Dead. Some questions focus on individual characters, while others test your memory of important storylines and locations.

The Clark Family

Questions may cover Madison Clark, Nick Clark, Alicia Clark and Travis Manawa, including their relationships, decisions and experiences during the early stages of the outbreak.

Daniel Salazar

The quiz includes details about Daniel Salazar, his family, his past and his role among the survivors throughout the series.

Victor Strand

Questions can cover Victor Strand, his relationships with other survivors and the communities and leadership positions he becomes involved with.

Morgan Jones

The quiz includes Morgan's arrival in Fear the Walking Dead, his relationships with other survivors and the communities and conflicts he becomes involved in.

Communities and Factions

Questions may cover locations and groups such as Broke Jaw Ranch, the Gonzalez Dam, the Proctors, the Stadium community, the Pioneers, the Tower and PADRE.

Major Story Events

The quiz covers major events including the early outbreak, the Mexico storyline, the dam conflict, the Stadium storyline, the nuclear missile launch, the Tower and the final PADRE storyline.

How Quiz Works

The quiz contains 50 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 50 questions in total, your correct answers are converted into a final percentage.

Your final result represents how many questions you answered correctly. The result categories are based on your performance across all 50 questions.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the Quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an Answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Select an answer to continue through the quiz.

Next and Back Buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review or change your selection. Your question number and progress are displayed as you move through the quiz.

Finishing the Quiz

Continue until all 50 questions have been answered. On the final question, the Next button changes to SUBMIT. Once every question has been answered, select SUBMIT to finish the quiz and view your final score.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result.

FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 50 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What seasons are covered?

The quiz covers all eight seasons of Fear the Walking Dead, from the beginning of the outbreak through the final season and PADRE storyline.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers characters, communities, locations, factions and major story events, including Madison, Nick, Alicia, Daniel, Strand, Morgan, John Dorie, the Pioneers, the Tower, PADRE and other important parts of the series.

Which series does this quiz cover?

This quiz focuses on Fear the Walking Dead, the post-apocalyptic series set within The Walking Dead universe.

Do I need to watch the entire series?

It is recommended. The questions cover events and characters from all eight seasons, including major developments that occur later in the series.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 50 questions you answered correctly. A higher percentage means you correctly remembered more details from the series.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share your result and see whether your friends can beat your score.
