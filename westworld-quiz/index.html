About the Westworld Quiz

Think you know everything about Westworld? Put your knowledge to the test with this 60-question trivia quiz covering Hosts, characters, parks, artificial intelligence, technology, identities, organizations and major events from the series.

The questions are designed for viewers who want to test how closely they remember the complicated world of Westworld. You may be asked about Dolores, Bernard, Maeve, William, Ford, the Man in Black and other important characters, as well as the different parks, Hosts and systems that shape the story.

Some questions focus on major storylines, while others test smaller details that can be easy to forget. Multiple timelines, changing identities, revelations and connections between characters make the series particularly challenging to remember.

Westworld Series Details

Series: Westworld

Genre: Science fiction, western, drama and thriller

Setting: Westworld and other Delos destinations, as well as locations outside the parks

Central concept: Artificially created Hosts designed to interact with human guests

Major themes: Artificial intelligence, consciousness, identity, free will and human behavior

Important technology: Hosts, artificial intelligence systems, control systems and Delos technology

Source material: The television series is inspired by Michael Crichton's 1973 film Westworld.

How the Westworld Quiz Works

The quiz contains 60 multiple-choice questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully before selecting your answer.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. Your correct answers are converted into a percentage, with a maximum score of 100%.

Your final result represents how many questions you answered correctly. The knowledge categories are based on your performance across all 60 questions.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the Quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an Answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Once you choose an answer, the quiz will continue to the next question.

Next and Back Buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review or change your selection. Your question number and progress are displayed as you move through the quiz.

Finishing the Quiz

Continue until all 60 questions have been answered. When you reach the final question, submit your answers to finish the quiz and view your final score and Westworld knowledge result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result.

Westworld Trivia and FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

The Westworld quiz contains 60 questions, with four possible choices for each question.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What does the quiz cover?

Questions cover Hosts, human characters, parks, artificial intelligence, technology, organizations, identities, relationships, locations and major events from Westworld.

Does the quiz cover all four seasons?

Yes. The quiz covers material from all four seasons, including characters, storylines, locations and developments introduced throughout the series.

Why can Westworld be difficult to remember?

The series uses multiple timelines, changing identities, Hosts with different roles and revelations that are gradually explained. Details that seem unimportant at first can become significant later, making the show especially challenging for trivia fans.

Is this quiz only about the characters?

No. Character questions are only part of the quiz. Other questions cover the parks, Hosts, artificial intelligence, technology, organizations, identities, locations and important plot details.

Do I need to watch the entire series?

It is recommended. The questions can include details from different parts of the series, including developments and revelations that become clear later.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 60 questions you answered correctly. A higher percentage means you correctly remembered more of the show's details.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your Westworld knowledge score.
