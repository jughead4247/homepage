Choose a Quiz

Browse quizzes covering survival scenarios, apocalyptic worlds, movies, television series, trivia, and fictional characters. Each quiz shows its question count, difficulty, and estimated completion time before you begin.

2

Test what you know, make decisions in fictional situations, or choose the answers that best represent what you would do. Some quizzes reward correct knowledge while others use choices to determine your final result.

Complete the quiz to see your score, survival outcome, character match, or other result. You can also retake many quizzes and experiment with different answers to see how your result changes.

ABOUT THE SITE

Apocalypse Quizzes is an interactive quiz website built around survival, apocalyptic scenarios, fictional worlds, movies, television series, trivia, and characters. The collection includes both short quizzes that can be completed in a few minutes and longer tests designed for fans who want a deeper challenge.

The quizzes generally fall into two broad experiences. Knowledge and trivia quizzes ask questions about characters, events, settings, objects, storylines, and other details from movies and television shows. Scenario and decision quizzes instead place you in fictional situations and ask you to decide what you would do.

Some quizzes combine these ideas with character-focused results, survival outcomes, or other scoring systems. Because each quiz is designed around its own subject and format, the number of questions, difficulty, scoring method, and final result can vary from one quiz to another.

The results are intended for entertainment, interactive storytelling, fandom, and casual self-discovery rather than formal testing or professional assessment.

EXPLORE DIFFERENT QUIZ TYPES

There is more than one way to take a quiz here. Some challenges ask what you would do when resources are limited, while others test whether you remember the details of a favorite movie or series. Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of quizzes are available?

Apocalypse Quizzes features several types of interactive quizzes, including survival and apocalypse scenarios, preparedness challenges, movie and TV quizzes, trivia, and fictional character quizzes. The collection includes both short quizzes and longer tests with more questions.

Are the quizzes free?

Yes. The quizzes on Apocalypse Quizzes are designed to be available online without requiring a paid subscription to take part.

Do I need an account to take a quiz?

No account is required to start a quiz. You can choose a quiz, answer the questions, and view your result directly through the website.

Are the survival quizzes real survival tests?

No. Survival and apocalypse scenarios are fictional entertainment experiences. They are designed around hypothetical situations and are not intended to predict how someone would perform during a real emergency or disaster.

How are quiz scores calculated?

Scoring depends on the individual quiz. Knowledge quizzes generally award points for correct answers, while decision-based quizzes may use different scoring systems to produce a survival result, character match, or other outcome. The scoring method can therefore vary between quizzes.

Are the quiz results scientifically accurate?

Not necessarily. Quiz scoring systems are created for the individual entertainment experience and should not be treated as professional psychological, personality, military, survival, or other formal assessments.

Are the movie and TV quizzes based on the actual source material?

The fan knowledge quizzes are based on details from their respective movies or television series, including characters, events, settings, objects, and storylines. Questions are intended to test knowledge of the source material rather than provide professional or official assessments.

Can I take the quizzes more than once?

Yes. You can retake quizzes and try different answers, particularly decision-based quizzes where changing your choices can affect the final result.

How often are new quizzes added?

The collection is regularly expanded with new quizzes covering different fictional worlds, movies, television series, survival scenarios, characters, and trivia subjects. New quizzes may vary in length, difficulty, and format depending on the topic.
