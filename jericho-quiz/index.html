About the Jericho TV Series Quiz

The Jericho TV Series Quiz is a 20-question trivia challenge based on the post-apocalyptic television series Jericho. The story follows Jake Green and the residents of Jericho, Kansas, after nuclear attacks devastate cities across the United States.

As the survivors struggle to protect their town, they face shortages, neighboring communities, armed groups, mercenaries and political changes caused by the collapse of the United States.

This quiz focuses on the show's characters, locations, factions, major events and important story details. It is designed for viewers who want to test how closely they remember the world of Jericho.

Jericho TV Series Details

Original run: 2006–2008

Genre: Post-apocalyptic drama, action and thriller

Main setting: Jericho, Kansas and the surrounding post-nuclear United States

Main character: Jake Green, played by Skeet Ulrich

Important characters: Robert Hawkins, Johnston Green, Eric Green, Emily Sullivan, Stanley Richmond and Dale Turner

Major story elements: The nuclear attacks, New Bern, Ravenwood, Jennings & Rall and the collapse of the United States government

What This Quiz Covers

The questions are based on events and details shown or established throughout Jericho. Some questions focus on characters and their relationships, while others test your memory of specific events and developments after the nuclear attacks.

Characters

Questions may cover Jake Green, Robert Hawkins, Johnston Green, Eric Green, Emily Sullivan, Stanley Richmond, Dale Turner, Jonah Prowse, John Goetz and other important characters.

The Nuclear Attacks

The quiz includes details about the attacks, the destruction of American cities and the uncertainty surrounding who was responsible.

Jericho and New Bern

The relationship between Jericho and New Bern becomes an important part of the story. Questions may test your memory of their conflicts, trade, resources and attempts to survive.

Ravenwood and Jennings & Rall

The series expands beyond the immediate survival of Jericho as powerful organizations and armed groups become involved in the aftermath of the nuclear attacks.

The New United States

The quiz also covers the political changes that follow the collapse of the United States, including competing governments and the new political order that develops.

How Quiz Works

The quiz contains 20 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 20 questions in total, your correct answers are converted into a final percentage.

Your final result represents how many questions you answered correctly. The result categories are based on your performance across the 20 questions.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the Quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an Answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Once you choose an answer, the quiz automatically continues to the next question.

Next and Back Buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review or change your selection. Your question number and progress are displayed as you move through the quiz.

Finishing the Quiz

Continue until all 20 questions have been answered. When you reach the end, submit your answers to finish the quiz and view your final score and Jericho knowledge result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result.

FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 20 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers Jericho, including Jake Green, Robert Hawkins, Johnston Green, Emily Sullivan, Stanley Richmond, Dale Turner, the nuclear attacks, New Bern, Ravenwood, Jennings & Rall and the post-nuclear political landscape.

Is this quiz only about the characters?

No. Character questions are only part of the quiz. Other questions cover the nuclear attacks, locations, resources, organizations, political developments and important story events.

Which series does this quiz cover?

This quiz focuses on the television series Jericho, which originally aired from 2006 to 2008.

Do I need to watch the series before taking the quiz?

It is recommended. The questions are based on events, characters and details from the series, including information that may be difficult to answer without having watched it.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 20 questions you answered correctly. A higher percentage means you correctly remembered more of the series' details.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share your result and see whether your friends can beat your score.
