About Zombie Survival Quiz

How would you react if a zombie outbreak happened today? This survival quiz puts you through a series of situations involving food, water, shelter, strangers, transportation and increasingly dangerous conditions.

Each question gives you four possible decisions. The choices have different survival values based on the strategy represented by each answer.

At the end of the quiz, your choices are converted into a survival score out of 100 and an estimated survival time.

How Does the Quiz Work?

The quiz contains 20 zombie-apocalypse survival scenarios. Each question gives you four possible choices.

Choose the option that best represents what you would actually do. Different choices contribute different numbers of survival points.

The maximum possible score is 100 points. Your final score determines the survival result and estimated survival time shown at the end.

What This Quiz Covers

The Outbreak

The opening scenarios focus on the first hours of an outbreak, gathering essentials, securing yourself and deciding when to leave.

Travel and Movement

You will face decisions involving abandoned vehicles, dangerous roads, unfamiliar terrain and changing zombie behavior.

Shelter and Health

Several scenarios involve finding shelter, treating injuries and deciding how carefully to inspect potentially dangerous locations.

Other Survivors

The quiz also examines how you deal with strangers, trading, group disagreements and larger survivor groups.

Resources

Food, water, medicine, transportation and other supplies become increasingly important as the situation continues.

Long-Term Survival

The final scenarios focus on settlements, security, leadership and building a sustainable way of surviving after the initial outbreak.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the Quiz

Select START QUIZ to begin. The information section disappears while you are answering the questions.

Selecting an Answer

Select one of the four choices. Your selected answer is highlighted and the quiz automatically advances to the next question.

Next and Back Buttons

The Back button allows you to review earlier questions. Your previous answers are remembered, so you can change a decision before submitting.

Finishing the Quiz

You must answer all 20 questions before the final result can be submitted.

Try Again

After seeing your result, select TRY AGAIN to restart the quiz with a fresh set of answers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 20 survival questions.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum possible survival score is 100.

How are the answers scored?

Each question has four choices worth between 0 and 5 survival points. The points are added together to produce your final score.

Is the survival time scientifically accurate?

No. The estimated survival time is part of the quiz result and is intended for entertainment. It is not a scientific prediction of how long a person would survive.

Can I change an answer?

Yes. Use the Back button to return to a previous question and select another answer.

Are there right and wrong answers?

The quiz assigns different survival values to each choice. These values represent the survival strategy used by the quiz.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start over.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the challenge button on the result screen to share the quiz and invite someone else to see how long they would survive.
