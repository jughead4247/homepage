About the Oblivion Movie Quiz

The Oblivion Movie Quiz is a 35-question trivia challenge based on the 2013 science-fiction film Oblivion. The story follows Jack Harper, a technician stationed on a devastated Earth, as he begins to uncover the truth about his mission, the supposed Scavs and the mysterious alien entity known as the Tet.

The movie combines science fiction, post-apocalyptic survival and mystery as Jack's understanding of the world around him begins to change. The discovery of Julia and the truth about the Odyssey mission lead to a series of revelations about what happened to humanity.

This quiz focuses on the movie's characters, locations, technology, spacecraft, mission history, clones, the Tet and major story revelations. It is designed for viewers who want to test how closely they remember the events of Oblivion.

Oblivion Movie Details

Release year: 2013

Director: Joseph Kosinski

Genre: Science fiction, action and adventure

Main setting: A devastated Earth in the year 2077

Main character: Jack Harper, played by Tom Cruise

Key technology: Autonomous drones, hydro rigs and the Tet

Key spacecraft: The Odyssey and Jack's Bubbleship

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 35 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers Oblivion, including Jack Harper, Victoria Olsen, Julia Rusakova, Malcolm Beech, Tech 52, the Odyssey mission, Tower 49, the drones, hydro rigs, the Tet, the Scavs and major story revelations.

Which movie does this quiz cover?

This quiz focuses on the 2013 science-fiction film Oblivion, directed by Joseph Kosinski and starring Tom Cruise as Jack Harper.

Do I need to watch the movie before taking the quiz?

It is recommended. The questions are based on events, characters and details from the movie, including information that may be difficult to answer without having watched it.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 35 questions you answered correctly. A higher percentage means you correctly remembered more of the movie's details.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz contains spoilers for Oblivion. Questions may reveal important details about Jack Harper, Julia, Malcolm Beech, the Scavs, the Odyssey mission, Tech 52, the Tet, the clones and other major events from the movie.

If you have not watched Oblivion, consider watching the movie before taking the quiz to avoid having important story details revealed.
