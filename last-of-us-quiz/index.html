About The Last of Us TV Show Quiz

The Last of Us TV Show Quiz is a 50-question trivia challenge based on the HBO television series The Last of Us. The story follows Joel Miller and Ellie Williams as they travel across a post-apocalyptic United States devastated by a fungal infection.

The series combines post-apocalyptic survival, drama, horror and character-driven storytelling. Joel and Ellie encounter survivors, hostile groups, infected creatures and difficult choices throughout their journey.

This quiz focuses on the show's characters, relationships, locations, groups, infected, important events and major story details. It is designed for viewers who want to test how closely they remember what happened throughout the series.

The Last of Us TV Show Details

Release year: 2023

Genre: Post-apocalyptic drama, horror and science fiction

Based on: The video game series developed by Naughty Dog

Main characters: Joel Miller and Ellie Williams

Main setting: A post-apocalyptic United States following a devastating fungal outbreak

Major groups: FEDRA, Fireflies, survivors and various hostile groups

Major threat: Infected humans affected by the Cordyceps fungus

What This Quiz Covers

The questions are based on details shown or established in The Last of Us television series. Some questions focus on characters and relationships, while others test your memory of locations, groups, infected and major events.

Joel and Ellie

Questions may cover Joel Miller, Ellie Williams, their backgrounds, relationship, journey and the events that shape their lives throughout the series.

Tess and Tommy

The Infected

Questions may test your knowledge of the infected, Cordyceps infection and the different threats encountered by Joel, Ellie and other survivors.

Locations and Major Events

The questions also cover important locations, journeys, encounters and major events that take place throughout the series.

How The Quiz Works

The quiz contains 50 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 2 points, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 50 questions in total, the maximum score is 100 points.

Your final score is converted into a percentage representing how many questions you answered correctly. The result categories are based on your performance across all 50 questions.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the Quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an Answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Once you choose an answer, the quiz will continue to the next question.

Next and Back Buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review or change your selection. Your question number and progress are displayed as you move through the quiz.

Finishing the Quiz

Continue until all 50 questions have been answered. When you reach the end, submit your answers to finish the quiz and view your final score and knowledge result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result.
