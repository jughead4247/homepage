About The Maze Runner Movie Quiz

The Maze Runner Movie Quiz is a 25-question trivia challenge for fans of the post-apocalyptic science-fiction movie The Maze Runner.

The film follows Thomas after he arrives in the Glade with no memory of his past and discovers a mysterious Maze surrounding the settlement.

The questions cover Thomas, Alby, Newt, Gally, Minho, Chuck, Teresa, the Glade, the Maze, the Grievers, the Changing, WCKD, the Flare and other major events from the movie.

The Maze Runner Movie Details

Title: The Maze Runner

Release Year: 2014

Genre: Science fiction, action, thriller

Main Character: Thomas

Setting: The Glade and the surrounding Maze

The movie follows a group of young people trapped in the Glade while a constantly changing Maze surrounds them.

What This Quiz Covers

Characters

Questions cover Thomas, Teresa, Newt, Alby, Gally, Minho, Chuck, Ben, Jeff and other important characters.

The Glade

Test your memory of the settlement, its different roles, rules and the way the Gladers organize their lives.

The Maze

Questions include the Maze sections, Runners, changing walls and the search for an escape route.

Grievers and the Changing

The quiz covers the dangerous creatures inside the Maze, Griever attacks and what happens after a Glader is stung.

WCKD and the Experiment

Some questions examine WCKD, the purpose of the Maze experiment, the Flare and the information revealed near the end of the movie.

How The Quiz Works

The quiz contains 25 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. Your final score is converted into a percentage.

Your result also shows your number of correct and incorrect answers, total questions and overall accuracy.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an answer

Read all four choices before making your selection. Once you choose an answer, the quiz moves forward automatically after a short delay.

Next and Back buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review your selection.

Finishing the quiz

Continue until all 25 questions have been answered. The final question displays a SUBMIT button once every question has been answered.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result with others.
