About The 100

The 100 is a post-apocalyptic science fiction television series developed by Jason Rothenberg. It premiered in 2014 and follows humanity's attempt to survive after a nuclear catastrophe has devastated Earth.

The story initially focuses on the survivors living aboard the Ark, a collection of space stations that became humanity's home after the disaster. When the Ark's resources and life-support systems become increasingly limited, 100 juvenile prisoners are sent to Earth to determine whether the planet is habitable.

The return to Earth introduces the young prisoners to dangers they were never prepared for, as well as communities of people who survived the apocalypse. Clarke Griffin, Bellamy Blake, Octavia Blake, Raven Reyes, Murphy and other characters become central to the conflicts that follow.

Over seven seasons, the series expands beyond the original conflict on Earth into new locations, societies and threats. The quiz therefore covers more than the show's early survival story, including major developments involving the Ark, Grounders, Mount Weather, Praimfaya, Sanctum and Bardo.

Characters You Should Know

Clarke Griffin

Clarke is one of the central characters of The 100. Her medical knowledge, leadership decisions and relationships with the different survivor groups make her important throughout the series.

Bellamy Blake

Bellamy is one of the original 100 and becomes a major leader among the people sent to Earth. His relationship with Clarke and his changing approach to leadership are important parts of the series.

Octavia Blake

Octavia begins the story as a member of the Ark's younger generation and develops through several radically different stages of the series. Her relationships with the Grounders and later communities make her particularly important to the show's world-building.

Raven Reyes

Raven is an engineer and mechanic whose technical abilities become valuable to the survivors. Her knowledge of machines, spacecraft and technology connects her to many of the show's major problems.

John Murphy

Murphy is one of the original 100. His character changes considerably throughout the series, and his relationships with the other survivors make him relevant to several major storylines.

The Grounders

The Grounders are descendants of people who survived on Earth after the nuclear apocalypse. Their clans, customs, leaders and conflicts with the people from the Ark become central to the middle portion of the series.

Important Story Elements

The Ark

The Ark is the space-based home of the survivors whose ancestors escaped the nuclear apocalypse. Limited resources and failing life-support systems eventually lead its leaders to consider sending the 100 to Earth.

Grounder Clans

The people who survived on Earth developed their own communities and cultures. The Grounder clans become one of the most important political and military forces encountered by the characters.

Mount Weather

Mount Weather becomes a major location and source of conflict after the 100 encounter another surviving community. The facility's resources, inhabitants and plans create one of the series' major conflicts.

Praimfaya

Praimfaya refers to the catastrophic consequences of nuclear radiation and later environmental disasters that repeatedly threaten humanity's ability to survive on Earth.

Sanctum

Sanctum becomes the principal setting for a later stage of the series. Its inhabitants, political system, religious beliefs and unusual history introduce a different type of conflict from the earlier Grounder wars.

Bardo

Bardo becomes important during the final season. The location is connected to the Disciples and the larger conflict involving the mysterious Anomaly Stone and travel between worlds.

Major Story Elements and Events

Spoilers: This section discusses major developments from across the series.

The 100 Reach Earth

The series begins with 100 juvenile prisoners being sent from the Ark to Earth to test whether the planet can support human life again. Their arrival quickly reveals that the world is not as empty as the Ark's inhabitants believed.

Conflict With the Grounders

Contact with the Grounders develops into a major conflict involving territory, resources, leadership and attempts at cooperation. The relationship between the two groups changes repeatedly as new threats appear.

Mount Weather

The discovery of Mount Weather introduces another surviving human population and creates a conflict involving the 100, the Mountain Men and the future of both communities.

The Second Nuclear Apocalypse

Later events force the survivors to confront another catastrophic threat to Earth's habitability. This changes where the characters can live and contributes to the transition between several major settings.

Sanctum and the Anomaly

Later seasons introduce Sanctum and eventually expand the story beyond a single planet. The Anomaly, the Disciples and Bardo become important to the final storyline.
