ABOUT THE QUIZ

The Tomorrow War Movie Quiz is a 30-question trivia challenge based on the 2021 science-fiction action film The Tomorrow War. The story follows Dan Forester, a former soldier and science teacher who is recruited into a future war after soldiers from the future arrive in the present with a warning about humanity's survival.

The movie combines science fiction, military action, time travel and alien survival as Dan joins the fight against the White Spikes. The story moves between the present day and a future devastated by the alien invasion.

This quiz focuses on the movie's characters, locations, military operations, time-travel technology, the White Spikes and major story events. It is designed for viewers who want to test how closely they remember the film.

The Tomorrow War Movie Details

Release year: 2021

Director: Chris McKay

Genre: Science fiction, action and military thriller

Main character: Dan Forester, played by Chris Pratt

Key character: Muri Forester, Dan's daughter in the future

Main threat: The White Spikes, an aggressive alien species that attacks humanity

Key technology: A time-travel connection that allows people from the future to communicate with and recruit people from the past

What This Quiz Covers

The questions are based on details shown or established in The Tomorrow War. Some questions focus on characters and their relationships, while others test your memory of the future war, the White Spikes and important events throughout the movie.

Characters

Questions may cover Dan Forester, Muri Forester, Charlie, Dorian, James Forester, Emmy, Colonel Forester and other important characters from the story.

The Future War

The quiz includes details about the future battlefield, humanity's struggle against the White Spikes and the missions carried out by soldiers recruited from the past.

The White Spikes

The White Spikes are central to the movie's conflict. Questions can test your memory of their appearance, behavior, abilities and the way humanity attempts to stop them.

Time Travel and the Jump

Time travel is one of the movie's key concepts. Questions may cover how people are transported between the present and future, why the connection exists and how it affects the war.

The Alien Threat

The movie gradually reveals more information about the origin and nature of the White Spikes. The quiz covers important discoveries and the attempts made to develop a way to defeat them.

Major Story Events

Questions may also cover important missions, discoveries, character relationships and other events that shape the outcome of the movie.

How Quiz Works

The quiz contains 30 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 30 questions in total, your correct answers are converted into a final percentage.

Your final result represents how many questions you answered correctly. The result categories are based on your performance across the 30 questions.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the Quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an Answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Once you choose an answer, the quiz will move forward automatically.

Next and Back Buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review or change your selection. Your question number and progress are displayed as you move through the quiz.

Finishing the Quiz

Continue until all 30 questions have been answered. When you reach the end, submit your answers to finish the quiz and view your final score and knowledge result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result.

FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 30 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers The Tomorrow War, including Dan Forester, Muri Forester, Charlie, Dorian, James Forester, the White Spikes, the future war, time travel and major story events.

Is this quiz only about the characters?

No. Character questions are only part of the quiz. Other questions cover the White Spikes, military missions, time travel, locations, discoveries and important plot details.

Which movie does this quiz cover?

This quiz focuses on the 2021 science-fiction action film The Tomorrow War, directed by Chris McKay and starring Chris Pratt as Dan Forester.

Do I need to watch the movie before taking the quiz?

It is recommended. The questions are based on events, characters and details from the movie, including information that may be difficult to answer without having watched it.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 30 questions you answered correctly. A higher percentage means you correctly remembered more of the movie's details.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz contains spoilers for The Tomorrow War. Questions may reveal important details about Dan Forester, Muri Forester, the White Spikes, the future war, time travel and other major events from the movie.
