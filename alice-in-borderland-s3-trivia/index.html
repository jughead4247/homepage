About The Quiz

The Alice in Borderland Season 3 Quiz is a 30-question trivia challenge based on the third season of the Japanese survival thriller Alice in Borderland.

Season 3 follows Arisu and Usagi as they are drawn back into the Borderland and become involved in a new series of dangerous games surrounding the mysterious Joker.

The quiz covers the season's characters, games, rules, clues, important events and details from the six-episode final season.

Alice in Borderland Season 3 Details

Season: 3

Episodes: 6

Release: 2025

Main characters: Arisu, Usagi, Banda, Ann, Yaba, Kuina and the new Season 3 characters

Important games: Old Maid, Sacred Fortunes, Zombie Hunt, Runaway Train, Tokyo Bingo Tower, Kick the Can and Future Sugoroku

Major mystery: The Joker and the nature of the Borderland

How Quiz Works

The quiz contains 30 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 30 questions in total, your correct answers are converted into a final percentage.

Your final result represents how many questions you answered correctly. The result categories are based on your performance across the 30 questions.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the Quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an Answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Once you choose an answer, continue to the next question.

Next and Back Buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and change your selection.

Finishing the Quiz

Continue until all 30 questions have been answered. When you reach the end, submit your answers to finish the quiz and view your final score and knowledge result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 30 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers Alice in Borderland Season 3, including Arisu, Usagi, Banda, Ann, the Watchman, the Joker, Zombie Hunt, Sacred Fortunes, Runaway Train, Tokyo Bingo Tower and Future Sugoroku.

Which season does this quiz cover?

This quiz focuses specifically on Season 3 of Alice in Borderland, rather than the events of Seasons 1 and 2.

Do I need to watch Season 3 before taking the quiz?

It is recommended. The questions are based on events, characters, games and details from Season 3, including major spoilers.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 30 questions you answered correctly. A higher percentage means you correctly remembered more details from Season 3.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz contains major spoilers for Alice in Borderland Season 3. Questions may reveal important details about Arisu, Usagi, the Joker, the Watchman and the games played throughout the season.

If you have not watched Season 3, consider watching it before taking the quiz to avoid having important story details revealed.
