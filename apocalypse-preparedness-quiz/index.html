About the Apocalypse Preparedness Quiz

What if the apocalypse started at this exact moment? This fictional preparedness quiz examines the advantages and limitations you would have based on your current situation.

The 30 questions consider areas such as your physical ability, medical preparedness, practical skills, available resources, living environment and ability to deal with changing circumstances.

Your answers are combined into a fictional preparedness score from 0% to 100%, along with a profile showing how your different preparedness areas compare.

The goal is not to determine whether you would actually survive an apocalypse. Instead, it creates a fictional snapshot of the advantages and limitations represented by your answers.

What This Quiz Measures

The quiz looks at several different areas that can influence fictional preparedness scenarios. Each category represents a different type of advantage or limitation.

Physical Ability

Questions in this category consider physical capability, mobility, endurance and your ability to handle physically demanding situations.

Medical Preparedness

This category considers access to medical supplies, basic health-related preparedness and your ability to deal with injuries or medical problems within the fictional scenario.

Practical Skills

Practical skills include useful abilities such as repairing equipment, handling tools, finding solutions to problems and performing everyday survival-related tasks.

Resources

Resources include useful supplies, food, water, equipment and other assets that could provide advantages in the fictional scenario.

Environment

Your surroundings can affect preparedness as well. This category considers factors such as your location, shelter, surroundings and access to useful facilities or resources.

How the Quiz Works

The quiz contains 30 questions. Each question presents several possible choices based on a fictional apocalypse or survival situation.

Choose the answer that most closely represents your actual situation, abilities or available resources. The test is designed around what you have and what you can realistically do now.

Your selections contribute to an overall preparedness percentage and separate category scores.

The result also identifies your strongest preparedness area and an area that could use improvement according to the fictional scoring system.

Quiz Navigation

Read each situation carefully and select the choice that best represents your current circumstances.

Sharing Your Result

Understanding Your Preparedness Result

Your final percentage represents the fictional preparedness score generated from your answers. It is not a measurement of real-world survival ability.

The Survivor Profile provides additional detail by separating your result into Physical, Medical, Practical Skills, Resources and Environment.

Your strongest area identifies the category in which your answers produced the highest result, while the area to improve identifies a category with a comparatively lower result.

The result is intended to make the test more interesting and easier to interpret rather than provide a scientific assessment of disaster preparedness.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 30 questions in total. Each question presents multiple choices based on a fictional preparedness scenario.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum preparedness result is 100%.

What does my score mean?

Your score represents the fictional level of preparedness generated from the answers you selected. It does not predict how you would perform during a real disaster.

What categories are included?

The Survivor Profile includes Physical, Medical, Practical Skills, Resources and Environment.

Is this a real survival test?

No. This is a fictional entertainment quiz. It does not scientifically predict whether someone would survive an actual disaster, apocalypse or emergency.

Are the questions based on a specific movie or television series?

No. Unlike the site's movie and television trivia quizzes, this test is a general fictional preparedness scenario rather than a quiz about one specific fictional universe.

Should I answer based on what I wish I had?

For the most meaningful result within the quiz's fictional scoring system, answer based on what you actually have and what you can realistically do right now.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and explore different answers.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and compare fictional preparedness results.

Fictional Preparedness Test

This quiz is designed for entertainment and fictional scenario exploration. It is not a professional disaster-preparedness assessment, medical assessment, fitness assessment or prediction of real-world survival.

Real emergencies can involve circumstances that cannot be predicted by a quiz, including changing hazards, injuries, weather, infrastructure failures, emergency services and many other factors.

The score and category results should therefore be treated as part of the fictional quiz experience, not as professional advice or a guarantee of how someone would perform during an actual emergency.
