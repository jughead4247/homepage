HOW THE QUIZ WORKS

The quiz contains 30 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. Your final score is converted into a percentage out of 100%.

Your result reflects how well you remember the characters, investigations, clues and major events of Dexter: Resurrection Season 1.
