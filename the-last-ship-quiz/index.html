About The Last Ship Quiz

The Last Ship Quiz is a 50-question trivia challenge for viewers of the television series The Last Ship. It tests how well you remember the characters, ships, factions, locations, the Red Flu pandemic and major events that shaped the series.

The questions cover all five seasons, including Captain Tom Chandler, Mike Slattery, Dr. Rachel Scott, the crew of the Nathan James, the Immunes, the Red Flu and the different groups encountered throughout the story.

Some questions focus on major characters and memorable events, while others test smaller details about the crew, their missions, relationships, enemies and the changing world around the Nathan James.

The Last Ship Series Details

Series: The Last Ship

Seasons: 5

Episodes: 56

Main setting: The USS Nathan James and a world devastated by the Red Flu pandemic.

The story follows the crew of the Nathan James as they search for a cure, protect survivors, confront rival factions and attempt to help rebuild civilization.

What This Quiz Covers

Characters

Questions cover Tom Chandler, Mike Slattery, Rachel Scott, Danny Green, Kara Green, Tex, Wolf Taylor, Takehaya, President Michener and many other characters.

Nathan James

Test your knowledge of the destroyer, its crew, missions, command structure and role throughout the pandemic.

The Red Flu

Questions cover the virus, its spread, the search for a cure, natural immunity and later developments affecting the surviving population.

Factions and Enemies

The quiz includes questions about the Immunes, Russian forces, pirates, Giorgio Vellek, Gran Colombia and other groups encountered throughout the series.

Major Events

Questions also cover important missions, character deaths, political changes, discoveries and major events across all five seasons.

How The Last Ship Quiz Works

The quiz contains 50 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 2 points, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 50 questions in total, the maximum possible score is 100 points.

Your final score is converted into a percentage and determines your final rank.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an answer

Select the answer you believe is correct. Your selection is saved as you progress through the quiz.

Next and Back buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and change your selection.

Finishing the quiz

Answer all 50 questions and submit the quiz on the final question to see your final score, accuracy and rank.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After seeing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result.

The Last Ship Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 50 questions, with four possible choices for each question.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100 points. Each correct answer is worth 2 points.

Does the quiz cover all five seasons?

Yes. The questions include characters, events and details from all five seasons of The Last Ship.

What subjects are included?

Questions cover Tom Chandler, Mike Slattery, Rachel Scott, the Nathan James crew, the Red Flu, factions, missions, major events and other details from the series.

What does my score mean?

Your score and accuracy show how many questions you answered correctly and determine your final rank.

Can I challenge my friends?
