ABOUT THE QUIZ

The Edge of Tomorrow Movie Quiz is a 30-question trivia challenge for fans of the 2014 science-fiction action movie Edge of Tomorrow. The film follows Major William Cage as he becomes trapped in a time loop during humanity's war against the alien Mimics.

The questions cover William Cage, Rita Vrataski, Master Sergeant Farell, the UDF, the Mimics, the Alpha, the Omega, Operation Downfall, the transponder and Cage's repeated attempts to change the outcome of the battle.

Some questions test major plot details, while others focus on smaller facts about the characters, military operations and Mimic strategy. If you remember Cage's repeated battles and Rita's role in helping him understand the time loop, this quiz gives you a chance to test your memory.

Edge of Tomorrow Movie Details

Title: Edge of Tomorrow

Release Year: 2014

Genre: Science fiction, action

Director: Doug Liman

Based on: The Japanese light novel All You Need Is Kill by Hiroshi Sakurazaka.

Main Characters: Major William Cage, Rita Vrataski, Master Sergeant Farell and the UDF soldiers fighting the Mimics.

The story follows Cage after he is forced into combat against the Mimics and becomes caught in a repeating cycle in which his actions can change the course of the battle.

WHAT THIS QUIZ COVERS

The questions cover important characters, locations, military operations, Mimic biology and major events from the movie.

Major William Cage

Questions about Cage's background, his involvement in the war, his first encounter with the Mimics and his attempts to use the time loop to change the outcome.

Rita Vrataski

Questions about Rita's experience fighting the Mimics, her connection to the time-loop phenomenon and her role in helping Cage understand what is happening.

The Mimics, Alpha and Omega

The quiz covers the Mimics and the unusual biological mechanism that allows the alien forces to anticipate humanity's movements. Questions also cover the Alpha, the Omega and their importance to the time loop.

Operation Downfall

Questions cover the military operation against the Mimics, the planned assault and the information Cage gains through repeated attempts to change the battle.

The Time Loop

Several questions focus on how Cage experiences the repeating day, how information is retained between attempts and how the time-loop mechanic affects the story.

HOW THE QUIZ WORKS

The quiz contains 30 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before continuing.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 30 questions in total, your number of correct answers is converted into a final percentage out of 100%.

Your final result represents your knowledge of the movie based on your performance. A high score means you successfully remembered many of the characters, events, locations and time-loop details featured in Edge of Tomorrow.
