About the World War Z Quiz

The World War Z Quiz is a 25-question trivia challenge for fans of the 2013 zombie action thriller movie World War Z. It tests how well you remember Gerry Lane, his family, the global outbreak, the infected and the major events of his mission.

The questions cover important characters including Gerry Lane, Karin Lane, Segen, Thierry Umutoni, Jurgen Warmbrunn and Dr. Andrew Fassbach. You will also be tested on locations such as Philadelphia, South Korea, Jerusalem and the WHO research facility.

Some questions focus on major moments from the movie, while others test smaller details that may be easier to forget. If you watched World War Z carefully, this quiz gives you a chance to find out how much you really remember.

World War Z Movie Details

World War Z is a 2013 zombie action film starring Brad Pitt as former United Nations investigator Gerry Lane.

The story follows Gerry as he travels across different parts of the world searching for a way to understand and survive the rapidly spreading outbreak.

The movie features major locations including Philadelphia, South Korea, Jerusalem and a World Health Organization research facility.

What This Quiz Covers

The questions cover characters, locations, the global outbreak, the infected, major events and important discoveries made during Gerry Lane's investigation.

You will encounter questions about Gerry's family, his journey around the world, the Israeli defenses, Segen, the WHO facility and the strategy discovered near the end of the movie.

How the World War Z Quiz Works

The quiz contains 25 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before continuing.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 25 questions in total, your number of correct answers is converted into a final percentage out of 100%.

Your final result represents your World War Z knowledge based on your performance in the quiz. A high score means you successfully remembered many of the characters, locations, events and important details from the movie.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your decision. Select the answer you believe is correct and continue through the quiz.

Next and Back buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review your selection. Your current question number and progress are displayed as you move through the quiz.

Finishing the quiz

Continue until you have answered all 25 questions. When you reach the end, submit your answers to finish the quiz and view your final percentage and World War Z Knowledge result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result and challenge other fans.

World War Z Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 25 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers characters, locations, the global outbreak, the infected, major events and other memorable details from World War Z.

Is this quiz about the 2013 movie?

Yes. The quiz is specifically focused on the events, characters and story details from the 2013 movie World War Z.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 25 questions you answered correctly. A higher score indicates stronger knowledge of World War Z.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.
