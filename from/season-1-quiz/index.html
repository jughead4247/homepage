ABOUT THE QUIZ

The FROM Season 1 Quiz is a 25-question trivia challenge for fans of the supernatural mystery horror series. The first season follows people trapped in a mysterious town from which they cannot escape.

The questions cover Boyd Stevens, the Matthews family, Jade Herrera, Victor, Sara, Kenny, Donna and other residents trying to understand the rules of their terrifying new home.

The quiz also tests memorable dialogue, character relationships, the creatures that emerge at night, the protective talismans, the radio experiment and major revelations from Season 1.

HOW THE QUIZ WORKS

The quiz contains 25 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. Your final score is converted into a percentage out of 100%.

Your result represents how well you remember the characters, mysteries and events of FROM Season 1.
