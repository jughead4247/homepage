Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared, and processed when you visit Apocalypse Quizzes and use its website features.

This policy applies to the entire website, including interactive quizzes, articles, survival guides, informational content, navigation features, and other services available through the website.

By using the website, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this policy, subject to applicable privacy laws and consent requirements.

Information We Collect

Apocalypse Quizzes does not require visitors to create an account, register, provide their name, or submit personal information in order to browse articles, read survival guides, or take quizzes.

However, certain information may be collected automatically through website technologies and third-party services used to operate, measure, maintain, and support the website.

Depending on the services used and their settings, this information may include:

  • Pages and content visited, including quizzes and articles.
  • General website usage, page interactions, and browsing activity on the website.
  • Browser type, device type, operating system, and technical information.
  • IP address and approximate geographic location.
  • Referring websites, access times, and similar technical information.
  • Cookie identifiers and information associated with advertising or analytics technologies.

The exact information collected depends on the services involved, their configurations, and your browser and privacy settings.

Quiz Answers and Results

Our quizzes use your selected answers to calculate and display results. Depending on the quiz, its scoring system and calculations may differ.

Quiz answers are generally processed by the corresponding quiz page to calculate your results. You are not required to provide your name, email address, or other personal information to complete a quiz.

We do not ask visitors to submit their individual quiz answers as personal information to use the website.

However, general information about page visits and interactions may be collected separately through analytics or advertising services. This is different from the answers you select within a quiz.

You should avoid entering sensitive personal information into quiz responses, contact messages, or other website fields.

Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information when contacting us through the Contact page.

This information may include your email address, name, message, and any other details you choose to include in your communication.

We use information provided through contact communications to read and respond to inquiries, suggestions, feedback, and website-related requests.

Please avoid sending passwords, financial details, sensitive personal information, or other confidential information through general contact communications.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Apocalypse Quizzes may use cookies and similar technologies through services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense.

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for purposes including:

  • Understanding how visitors use the website.
  • Measuring website traffic and performance.
  • Supporting advertising and advertisement measurement.
  • Managing advertising frequency and relevance.
  • Supporting the operation and security of third-party services.

Third-party services may use first-party cookies, third-party cookies, browser storage, or other technologies depending on their configuration and the visitor's settings.

You can manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect how some third-party services operate.

Cookie and advertising technology requirements may vary depending on your location. Where applicable laws require consent for non-essential cookies or similar technologies, the necessary consent requirements must also be addressed. This Privacy Policy does not replace a consent mechanism where one is legally required.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with Apocalypse Quizzes and to evaluate website traffic and performance.

Google Analytics may collect information such as page views, website interactions, browser and device information, approximate location, referring sources, and other usage information.

Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies to recognize browsers and measure activity across website visits, depending on its configuration and available privacy settings.

Analytics information helps us understand which pages and topics visitors find useful and how the website can be improved.

Google processes information collected through its services according to its own applicable privacy policies and terms.

For more information, visit Google's information about safeguarding data in Google Analytics .

Advertising and Google AdSense

Apocalypse Quizzes uses Google AdSense and may display advertisements through Google and participating third-party advertising providers.

Advertising services may use cookies, web beacons, browser storage, IP addresses, or similar technologies to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent repeated advertisements, and support advertising-related services.

Depending on your location, consent choices, Google account settings, browser settings, and available advertising features, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Personalized advertising may use information associated with browsing activity, previous website visits, interests, or other available advertising signals to make advertisements more relevant.

Non-personalized advertisements may still use certain information and cookies for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting, and fraud prevention.

Google and participating advertising technology providers may process information according to their applicable privacy policies and advertising settings.

You can manage Google's personalized advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings .

Changing this setting does not necessarily disable advertisements. It controls Google's use of information for personalized advertising where the setting applies.

For more information, visit Google's explanation of how AdSense uses cookies .

Third-Party Services

To operate, maintain, measure, and support the website, we may rely on third-party services, including analytics providers, advertising providers, website hosting services, and other technical service providers.

These services may collect or process information when you visit the website or interact with features that depend on them.

Third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies, terms, and applicable data protection requirements.

We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers. Their services, technologies, and policies may change over time.

Google Privacy and Data Use

Google provides information about how it collects and uses data when people visit websites and applications that use Google services, including advertising and measurement products.

For more information, please review Google's Business Data Responsibility and Google's Privacy Policy .

How Information May Be Shared

Information may be processed or shared with third parties where necessary to operate the website and provide its services.

This may include sharing or making information available to:
