About the Love and Monsters Quiz

Think you know Love and Monsters? This 30-question movie quiz tests how closely you remember Joel Dawson's journey through a monster-filled post-apocalyptic world. The questions cover characters, creatures, survivor colonies, locations, relationships, equipment and memorable events from the movie.

You may encounter questions about Joel, Aimee, Boy, Clyde, Minnow, the underground colony and other important parts of the story. Some questions focus on major plot points, while others test smaller details that can be easy to forget after watching the movie only once.

The quiz is designed for fans who want to test their memory rather than their real-world survival skills. Whether you watched Love and Monsters recently or remember it from an earlier viewing, the questions give you a chance to see how much of the movie you still remember.

Love and Monsters Movie Details

Release year: 2020

Director: Michael Matthews

Genre: Science fiction, adventure, comedy, romance

Main character: Joel Dawson, played by Dylan O'Brien

Main characters: Joel Dawson, Aimee, Clyde, Minnow, Boy and Mav1s

Setting: A post-apocalyptic world inhabited by giant mutated creatures.

How the Love and Monsters Quiz Works

The quiz contains 30 multiple-choice questions. Every question provides four possible answers, so read each question carefully before making your selection. The questions cover different parts of the movie instead of concentrating on only one character or section of Joel's journey.

Each correct answer is worth 1 point, giving the quiz a maximum possible score of 30 points. Your final result is converted into a percentage and used to determine your Love and Monsters Knowledge level.

There is no penalty for taking the quiz again. If you finish with a lower score than expected, you can use the TRY AGAIN button and attempt the questions again. The quiz is intended as entertainment and movie trivia, so your result does not represent any official ranking or assessment.

Quiz Navigation

Select START QUIZ to begin. As you move through the questions, choose the answer you believe is correct. The progress indicator shows where you are in the 30-question quiz.

The navigation controls allow you to move between questions. Use Next to continue and Back to return to an earlier question when available. Your previously selected answers are preserved when you move backward.

After completing the quiz, your result screen displays your percentage, score breakdown and Love and Monsters Knowledge level. You can select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz, use SHARE MY RESULT to share your performance, or choose CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS.
