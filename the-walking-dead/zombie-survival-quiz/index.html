ABOUT THE QUIZ

Could You Survive The Walking Dead? is a 30-question zombie survival quiz inspired by the television series. It combines questions about walker behavior and familiar communities with practical decisions that could matter during a large-scale outbreak.

The quiz covers how walkers behave, what can make them dangerous, how herds and unusual variants may affect a survivor group, and how to approach encounters with other people and settlements.

It also includes general apocalypse survival topics such as choosing a long-term base, planning supply runs, maintaining food and water sources, and using fuel-powered generators safely.

The Walking Dead Details

Series: The Walking Dead

Coverage: Main television series, including events through Season 11

Seasons: 11

Genre: Post-apocalyptic horror, survival, drama and thriller

Central Conflict: Survivors must contend with the dead, scarce resources, dangerous groups and the difficult choices involved in building communities after civilization collapses.

Major Story Elements: Walker behavior, herds, variant walkers, the Whisperers, the Saviors, Terminus, Alexandria, the Commonwealth and other communities encountered throughout the series.

WHAT THIS QUIZ COVERS

The questions combine The Walking Dead story details with practical survival situations. They test both your familiarity with the series and your ability to think through common problems in a zombie apocalypse.

Walker Behavior

Consider how walkers react to living prey, sounds, injuries, weather and their surroundings. The quiz also touches on walker camouflage, severed heads and unusual climbing behavior.

Survival Decisions

Make choices about selecting a long-term base, escaping an approaching herd, searching unfamiliar buildings, assessing strangers and organizing supply runs.

Communities and Factions

Identify groups and settlements from the series, including Terminus, the Saviors, Alexandria, Woodbury, the Commonwealth, the Wolves and the Claimers.

Food, Water and Preparedness

The quiz considers long-term food production, illness reporting, water treatment and the safe placement of portable fuel-powered generators.

General Apocalypse Survival

A zombie outbreak would create problems beyond the walkers themselves. Planning, reliable resources, safe shelter and community cooperation would all influence the chances of survival.

HOW THE QUIZ WORKS

The quiz contains 30 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. Your final score is converted into a percentage out of 100%.

Your result reflects how well you know the series' walkers and communities, along with your choices in general survival situations.

Quiz Navigation
