ABOUT THE QUIZ

The War for the Planet of the Apes Quiz is a 30-question trivia challenge for fans of the 2017 post-apocalyptic science-fiction film. The movie follows Caesar as he leads his ape colony through a dangerous conflict with human survivors commanded by the Colonel.

The questions cover Caesar, Blue Eyes, Cornelius, Cornelia, Maurice, Rocket, Luca, Winter, Nova, Bad Ape, Donkey, the Colonel and the Alpha-Omega forces.

Some questions test major story events, while others focus on character relationships, locations, important discoveries and smaller details from the movie.

War for the Planet of the Apes Movie Details

Title: War for the Planet of the Apes

Release Year: 2017

Genre: Post-apocalyptic science-fiction action drama

Main Character: Caesar

Main Human Antagonist: The Colonel

The story follows Caesar and his fellow apes as they attempt to survive an increasingly violent conflict with human military forces. After the Colonel attacks Caesar's home and kills members of his family, Caesar sets out to find him.

WHAT THIS QUIZ COVERS

The questions cover important characters, relationships, locations and events from War for the Planet of the Apes.

Caesar and His Family

Questions cover Caesar's sons Blue Eyes and Cornelius, his mate Cornelia and the events that lead Caesar to pursue the Colonel.

Caesar's Mission

The quiz covers the apes who accompany Caesar on his mission, Winter's betrayal and the journey toward the Alpha-Omega facility.

Nova and Bad Ape

Questions cover Nova, her dog Boy, the reason she cannot speak and the unusual intelligent ape known as Bad Ape.

The Colonel and Alpha-Omega

The quiz covers the Colonel, Alpha-Omega, the captured apes, the relocation camp and the conflict with the Northern Army.

The Final Journey

Questions also cover Caesar's confrontation with the Colonel, the final events of the conflict and the oasis that becomes a new home for the apes.

HOW THE QUIZ WORKS

The quiz contains 30 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before continuing.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, while an incorrect answer gives you 0 points. With 30 questions in total, your number of correct answers is converted into a final percentage out of 100%.

Your final result represents your knowledge of War for the Planet of the Apes based on your performance. A high score means you successfully remembered many of the characters, events and details featured in the film.

FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 30 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum result is 100%. Each correct answer contributes 1 point before the score is converted into a percentage.

What does my score mean?

Your percentage shows how many of the 30 War for the Planet of the Apes questions you answered correctly. A higher score indicates that you remembered more of the characters, events and details covered by the quiz.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.
