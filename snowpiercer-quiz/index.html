About The Snowpiercer Quiz

The Snowpiercer Quiz is a 50-question trivia challenge based on the television series Snowpiercer. The quiz tests your knowledge of the characters, factions, events and changing world of the series.

The story takes place in a frozen world where the remaining survivors live aboard Snowpiercer, a massive train that continuously travels around the planet.

This quiz focuses on major characters and story details, including Layton, Melanie Cavill, Mr. Wilford, Snowpiercer, Big Alice, the Freeze, New Eden and important events throughout the series.

Snowpiercer TV Series Details

Format: Post-apocalyptic science-fiction television series

Main setting: Snowpiercer, a continuously moving train carrying the surviving population.

Major characters: Andre Layton, Melanie Cavill and Mr. Wilford are among the central figures featured in the series.

Key locations: Snowpiercer, Big Alice and New Eden.

Major threat: The frozen world created by the Freeze and the struggle for survival among the remaining humans.

What This Quiz Covers

The questions are based on events, characters, locations and story details from Snowpiercer. Some questions test your memory of individual characters, while others focus on the train, its society and the changing world outside.

Characters

Questions may cover Layton, Melanie Cavill, Mr. Wilford and other important characters, including their relationships, decisions and roles within the story.

Snowpiercer

The quiz includes questions about the train, its different sections, its social structure, important locations and the people who live aboard it.

Big Alice

Questions can test your memory of Big Alice, its role in the story and its relationship with Snowpiercer.

The Freeze

The frozen world outside the train is central to the series. The quiz covers events and story details connected to the Freeze and humanity's struggle to survive it.

New Eden

Questions may also cover New Eden and the events surrounding the survivors' changing hopes for life beyond the train.

Major Story Events

The quiz includes important events and developments from the series, including conflicts between characters and groups, changes aboard the trains and major turning points in the story.

How The Snowpiercer Quiz Works

The quiz contains 50 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct.

Each correct answer contributes to your final score. With 50 questions and 2 points available for each correct answer, the maximum score is 100.

Your final result shows your score out of 100, the number of questions you answered correctly, your incorrect answers and your overall accuracy.

Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before moving through the quiz.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the Quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The first question will appear with four possible answers.

Selecting an Answer

Read the question and all four choices before making your selection. Select the answer you believe is correct.

Next and Back Buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review or change your selection. Your question number and progress are displayed as you move through the quiz.

Finishing the Quiz

Continue until all 50 questions have been answered. When you reach the final question, submit your completed answers to finish the quiz and view your result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After viewing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz and attempt a higher score. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result.

FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 50 questions in total, and every question has four possible choices.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100. Each correct answer contributes 2 points, while an incorrect answer contributes 0 points.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers Snowpiercer, including Layton, Melanie Cavill, Mr. Wilford, Snowpiercer, Big Alice, the Freeze, New Eden, important characters, factions and major story events.

Is this quiz only about the characters?

No. Character questions are only part of the quiz. Other questions cover Snowpiercer, Big Alice, the Freeze, New Eden, conflicts, events and important story details.

Do I need to watch Snowpiercer before taking the quiz?

It is recommended. The questions are based on events, characters and details from the series, including information that may be difficult to answer without watching it.

What does my score mean?

Your score out of 100 reflects how many of the 50 questions you answered correctly. The result also shows your accuracy and the number of correct and incorrect answers.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Select CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see whether your friends can beat your score.
