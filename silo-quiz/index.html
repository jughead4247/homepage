About the Silo Quiz

The Silo Quiz is a 70-question trivia challenge for viewers of the Apple TV+ series Silo. It tests how well you remember the characters, events, locations, departments, technology, relics and mysteries revealed throughout the series.

The questions cover material from Seasons 1, 2 and 3, including major story developments as well as smaller details that can be easy to forget. The quiz is designed for both casual viewers and dedicated fans who want to see how much they remember.

Some questions focus on characters and major events, while others require knowledge of the Silos, their history, important objects, departments and revelations connected to the wider story.

Silo Series Details

Series: Silo

Release platform: Apple TV+

Genre: Science fiction, dystopian drama and mystery

Main character: Juliette Nichols, played by Rebecca Ferguson

Main setting: An underground silo containing a large enclosed society

Central mystery: Why the Silos exist and what lies beyond their walls

Key subjects: Silos, relics, departments, technology, the Pact, cleaning and the history of the outside world

What This Quiz Covers

The quiz covers characters, locations, departments, technology, relics, important events and story revelations from the first three seasons of Silo.

Characters

Questions about Juliette Nichols, Holston Becker, Bernard Holland, Martha Walker, George Wilkins, Solo, Robert Sims and other important characters.

The Silos

Questions about the underground communities, Silo 17, Silo 18 and information revealed about the wider Silo system.

Departments and Society

Test your knowledge of Mechanical, IT, Judicial, Supply, Mining and other departments and their roles within Silo society.

Technology and Relics

The quiz includes questions about relics, equipment, surveillance systems, technology and objects connected to the history of the Silos.

Major Mysteries and Revelations

Questions also cover discoveries about the Silos, the outside world, hidden information and major revelations from Seasons 1, 2 and 3.

How the Silo Quiz Works

The quiz contains 70 questions. Every question has four possible choices, but only one answer is correct. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct.

A correct answer gives you 1 point. An incorrect answer gives you 0 points. Your score is calculated from the number of questions you answer correctly and converted into a percentage out of 100.

A perfect result means you answered all 70 questions correctly. Your final result also includes a Silo Knowledge level based on your performance.

Quiz Navigation

Starting the Quiz

Select START QUIZ on the opening screen to begin. The quiz will then display the first question and its four possible answers.

Selecting an Answer

Select the answer you think is correct. Your selected option is highlighted so you can clearly see your current choice before continuing.

Next and Back Buttons

Use Next to move forward through the questions. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question and review or change your selection. Your progress is shown at the top of the quiz.

Finishing the Quiz

Continue answering the questions until you reach the final question. After all questions have been answered, use the submit option to finish the quiz and view your result.

Try Again and Challenge Friends

After seeing your result, select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz. You can also use SHARE MY RESULT or CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to share your result and compare your knowledge with other fans.

Silo Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

There are 70 questions, with four possible choices for each question.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum is 70 correct answers, which is displayed as a perfect 100% score.

Which seasons are covered?

The quiz covers Seasons 1, 2 and 3 of Silo.

What subjects are included?

Questions cover characters, Silos, departments, locations, relics, technology, major events and important story revelations.

Is the quiz only about Juliette?

No. The quiz covers Juliette as well as many other characters, departments, locations, mysteries and events from the series.

Do I need to watch all three seasons?

The quiz includes material from Seasons 1, 2 and 3, so watching all three seasons will give you the information needed for the full range of questions.

What does my score mean?

Your score represents the percentage of questions you answered correctly. The result also assigns a Silo Knowledge level based on your performance.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to start another attempt and try to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS after completing the quiz to share the challenge and see who remembers more about Silo.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz contains spoilers for Silo Seasons 1, 2 and 3. Some questions reveal important characters, events, relationships, discoveries, locations and story developments.
