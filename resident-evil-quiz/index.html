About The Resident Evil Movie Quiz

The Resident Evil Movie Quiz is a 25-question trivia challenge for fans of the live-action Resident Evil film series. It tests how well you remember Alice, Umbrella Corporation, the T-virus, creatures, facilities, characters and major events from the movies.

The questions include major story developments as well as smaller details that can be easy to forget. You may be asked about characters and their identities, Umbrella facilities, experiments, artificial intelligence, creatures, important locations and events that changed the course of the story.

Each question has four possible answers, with one correct choice. Every correct answer earns 2 points, giving the quiz a maximum score of 50. Your final result shows your score, number of correct answers, accuracy and Resident Evil Knowledge level.

The quiz focuses on the original live-action movie continuity starring Milla Jovovich as Alice rather than the Resident Evil video games. Although the movies are inspired by the games, they have their own characters, storylines and interpretations of the Resident Evil universe.

Resident Evil Movie Series Details

Original film series: Six live-action movies starring Milla Jovovich as Alice

Central character: Alice, portrayed by Milla Jovovich

Main organization: Umbrella Corporation

Major fictional pathogen: T-virus

Important facility: The Hive

Recurring technology: Artificial intelligence, cloning and biotechnology

Major threats: Infected humans, mutated creatures and Umbrella's biological weapons

What This Quiz Covers

The questions cover characters, organizations, locations, creatures, experiments and major events from the original live-action Resident Evil movies.

Alice

Questions can cover Alice's background, her connection to Umbrella, her exposure to the T-virus and the changes she experiences throughout the movie series.

Umbrella Corporation

Umbrella's facilities, executives, experiments and biological research are important parts of the movie storyline.

The T-virus

The fictional T-virus is central to the outbreak, infected creatures and many of the biological experiments shown throughout the films.

Characters

Questions may involve Alice, Matt Addison, Spence Parks, Carlos Oliveira, Claire Redfield, Chris Redfield, Albert Wesker and other important characters.

Creatures

The quiz can test your memory of creatures such as Cerberus, Lickers and Nemesis and the circumstances surrounding their appearances.

Major Events

Questions can cover important events across the six original live-action movies, including outbreaks, Umbrella experiments, survivor groups and major confrontations.

How The Resident Evil Movie Quiz Works

The quiz contains 25 multiple-choice questions. Each question presents four possible choices, with one correct answer.

Every correct answer is worth 2 points. With 25 questions, the maximum possible score is 50.

The quiz automatically moves to the next question shortly after you select an answer. You can also use the Back button to return to an earlier question and change your selection.

Your final result shows your score out of 50 together with your number of correct and incorrect answers and your overall accuracy percentage.

The quiz is designed for entertainment and fan trivia. It is not an official quiz produced by the filmmakers, production companies or rights holders of the Resident Evil franchise.

Resident Evil Movie Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

The quiz contains 25 multiple-choice questions, with four possible answers for each question.

What is the maximum score?

Each correct answer is worth 2 points, giving the quiz a maximum possible score of 50.

Which Resident Evil movies does the quiz cover?

The quiz focuses on the original six live-action Resident Evil movies starring Milla Jovovich as Alice.

Is the quiz based on the video games?

The main focus is the live-action movie continuity. While the movies were inspired by the Resident Evil games, the movie series has important differences in characters, storylines and events.

Who is Alice?

Alice is the central protagonist of the original live-action Resident Evil movie series and is portrayed by Milla Jovovich.

What is Umbrella Corporation?

Umbrella Corporation is a major organization in the movie series whose research, biotechnology and experiments are closely connected to many of the events experienced by Alice and the other survivors.

What is the T-virus?

The T-virus is a fictional biological agent in the Resident Evil universe. Its effects are responsible for many of the infected humans and creatures seen throughout the movies.

What is the Hive?

The Hive is an underground Umbrella facility beneath Raccoon City where important T-virus research takes place in the first Resident Evil movie.

Does the quiz include creatures?

Yes. Questions can cover creatures and infected enemies that appear throughout the movies, including Cerberus, Lickers and Nemesis.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to restart the quiz and attempt a higher score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS button after completing the quiz to share the challenge with other Resident Evil fans.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz contains spoilers for the original live-action Resident Evil movies, including character identities, organizations, creatures, locations, experiments and major story events.
