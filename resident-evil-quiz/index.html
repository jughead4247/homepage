About the Resident Evil (2002) Quiz

The Resident Evil (2002) Quiz is a 30-question trivia challenge focused on the original live-action film directed by Paul W. S. Anderson. It tests how well you remember Alice's awakening, the Umbrella Corporation, the Hive and the outbreak that turns the underground facility into a deadly trap.

Questions cover the Red Queen's security systems, the commando team's attempt to shut her down, the laser corridor, infected employees, the mutant creature known as the Licker, and the secrets surrounding Alice, Spence Parks and Matt Addison.

Each question has four possible answers, with one correct choice. Every correct answer earns 2 points, giving the quiz a maximum score of 60. Your final result shows your score, correct and incorrect answers, accuracy and Resident Evil knowledge level.

This quiz focuses specifically on the events of the 2002 movie, rather than the later sequels or the Resident Evil video games. The film establishes its own version of Alice's story while drawing on the franchise's outbreak, biotechnology and horror themes.

Resident Evil (2002) Movie Details

Release year: 2002

Director and writer: Paul W. S. Anderson

Central character: Alice, portrayed by Milla Jovovich

Main organization: Umbrella Corporation

Major fictional pathogen: T-virus

Primary setting: The Hive, an underground Umbrella research facility beneath Raccoon City

Key artificial intelligence: The Red Queen

Major threats: Infected employees, infected Dobermans and a dangerous mutant creature

What This Quiz Covers

The questions focus on the characters, locations, technology, infected creatures and plot revelations in the first Resident Evil movie. They mix straightforward facts with details from key scenes.

Alice and Matt

Test your memory of Alice's loss of memory, her connection to the Hive and her role in Lisa Addison's plan, along with Matt's reasons for investigating Umbrella.

Umbrella Corporation and the Hive

Questions cover the secret underground facility, the Red Queen, the commando team's mission and the security systems that become dangerous when the facility is shut down.

The T-virus and Spence Parks

The T-virus drives the film's outbreak. The quiz tests what Spence did, why he stole the virus and how the incident connects to the events inside the Hive.

Infected Creatures

Remember the infected Dobermans, reanimated Hive employees and the powerful mutant creature that threatens the surviving group.

Major Scenes and the Ending

Questions also cover the laser corridor, the escape by underground train, Rain's infection, Matt's injury and Alice's awakening after Umbrella takes control of the situation.

How the Resident Evil (2002) Quiz Works

The quiz contains 30 multiple-choice questions. Each question presents four possible choices, with one correct answer.

Every correct answer is worth 2 points. With 30 questions, the maximum possible score is 60.

After you select an answer, the quiz advances automatically after a short pause. You can also use the Back button to return to an earlier question and change your selection.

Your final result shows your score out of 60, the number of correct and incorrect answers, and your overall accuracy percentage.

This is an unofficial fan trivia quiz created for entertainment. It is not produced by or affiliated with the filmmakers or rights holders of the Resident Evil franchise.

Resident Evil (2002) Quiz FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

The quiz contains 30 multiple-choice questions, with four possible answers for each question.

What is the maximum score?

Each correct answer is worth 2 points, giving the quiz a maximum possible score of 60.

Which Resident Evil movie does this quiz cover?

It focuses on the original Resident Evil movie released in 2002, including the Hive outbreak and the events surrounding Alice.

Is the quiz based on the video games?

The questions focus on the 2002 live-action movie, not the video games. The film adapts ideas from the franchise but tells its own version of events.

Who is Alice?

Alice is the central character in the film, portrayed by Milla Jovovich. She awakens in a mansion with little memory and becomes involved in the effort to escape the Hive.

What is Umbrella Corporation?

Umbrella is the corporation operating the secret research facility known as the Hive. Its biological research and security systems are central to the outbreak in the film.

What is the T-virus?

The T-virus is a fictional biological agent linked to the infected humans and creatures encountered by the characters.

What is the Hive?

The Hive is Umbrella's underground research facility beneath Raccoon City, where the T-virus outbreak takes place.

Who is the Red Queen?

The Red Queen is the artificial intelligence that controls the Hive's security systems and tries to contain the outbreak.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to restart the quiz and attempt to earn a higher score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS button after completing the quiz to share it with other Resident Evil fans.
